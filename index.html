FAQ

What’s inside a mystery box?

A curated mix of themed goods chosen for the size and theme you pick. Contents are a surprise and change from order to order. Photos and theme descriptions illustrate a mood; they don’t guarantee specific products, brands, or quantities. Each box contains only new, genuine items purchased from authorized retailers. We don’t publish packing lists in advance; that’s the fun of it.

How do themes work?

You choose a theme from the list when you build your box, and we select items that fit that interest. Theme names describe a mood or hobby — they are not official brand shops, and Mystery Creations is not affiliated with or endorsed by any brand. Items are genuine, new products purchased from authorized retailers.

Is the price fixed? Is this a game of chance?

Every box is a fixed price for a fixed tier and theme you choose. What you pay is what you get: a curated themed gift box. There are no random prizes, odds, chances, rarity tiers, sweepstakes, or resale, trade-in, or buyback programs.

Can I choose specific items?

No — every box is a surprise. You choose the size and the theme, and we do the rest. If you have an allergy or restriction, write us on the contact form and we’ll do our best to accommodate.

What does the “who this box is for” field do?

At checkout you can optionally tell us a little about who the box is for. We’ll use it as a guide, but we can’t guarantee specific requests, brands, or items, and we aren’t obligated to fulfill every ask. That field is a non-binding guide, as described in our terms.

Can I send a box as a gift?

Yes. At checkout, enter the recipient’s U.S. shipping address so the box goes to them. You can add an optional gift note (up to 200 characters) to tuck in the box, and optionally tell us a little about who it’s for so we can use that as a guide when we pack it.

Can I include a gift note?

Yes. At checkout you can add an optional gift note (up to 200 characters) to tuck in the box, and an optional note about who it’s for. The “who it’s for” note is a guide only; we can’t guarantee specific requests or items.

How does shipping work?

We process and pack orders in 3–5 business days, then ship within the United States only, via UPS. Tracking is emailed to you when the package is on its way. Shipping is included in the box price — there is no extra shipping fee at checkout. Typical transit time is 1–5 business days after the box ships. Please enter a complete, accurate U.S. shipping address at checkout.

Can I change or cancel an order?

You may cancel for a full refund any time before the order ships. Write us at help@mymysterycreations.com as soon as you can. After a box has shipped, we don’t accept change-of-mind returns or changes to the theme, note, or address.

What’s your return and refund policy?

Because contents are a surprise, we don’t accept change-of-mind returns. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days. You may cancel for a full refund any time before the order ships. The full policy is in our terms of use.

What if my box arrives damaged or items are missing?

If a box arrives damaged, is missing items, or is not as described (wrong tier or theme), contact us within 14 days of delivery and include a photo. You can choose a replacement or a full refund. If a package is lost in transit, we’ll file a UPS claim and then replace the box or refund you.

How are payments handled?

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. You’ll pay the fixed price for the box you chose, and shipping is included. After payment, Stripe emails a receipt, and you’ll return to our thank-you page.

Are you affiliated with any brands?

No. Theme names describe interests only. Items are genuine, new products purchased from authorized retailers; Mystery Creations is not affiliated with or endorsed by any brand.

How do I get in touch?

Write us at help@mymysterycreations.com or use the contact form on this page. We also use that address for questions about an order, a return, or a review.
