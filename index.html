A few details

FAQ

What’s inside a mystery box?

A curated mix of themed goods chosen for the size and theme you pick. Contents are a surprise and change from order to order. Boxes include officially licensed products and/or generic themed items — never counterfeit or unofficial character merchandise. We don’t publish packing lists in advance; that’s the fun of it.

How does shipping work?

After you check out, we curate and pack your box, then ship it to the address you enter in Stripe Checkout. You’ll receive tracking (UPS) by email once the package is on its way. Shipping times and any shipping fees will be confirmed when payments go live; this page is a placeholder until rates are finalized.

Can I choose specific items?

No — every box is a surprise. You choose the size and the theme, and we do the rest. If you have an allergy, restriction, or a gift note, mention it in your order comments or on the contact form and we’ll do our best to accommodate.

What’s your return policy?

Because each box is custom-curated, returns are limited. If something arrives damaged or clearly not as described, contact us within 14 days and we’ll make it right. A full return policy will be published here before orders open. This is a placeholder until Stripe and shipping are live.

Are you affiliated with the brands in the theme names?

No. Theme names describe fandoms and interests only. Mystery Creations is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any studio, publisher, league, artist, or company mentioned in our themes.

When can I place an order?

The storefront is ready to browse. Secure checkout will open as soon as Stripe is connected. Your size and theme selection can be saved on this device in the meantime.
