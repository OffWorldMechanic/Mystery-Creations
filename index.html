A few details

FAQ

What’s the difference between a Mystery Gift and a Mystery Box?

A Mystery Gift is one single surprise item, not a box. It costs $10, $15, $20, or $25, ships free and directly from a retailer to the recipient as a gift with no price inside, and usually arrives within 2–7 business days. A Mystery Box contains multiple themed gifts, hand-packed in one box at our shop with a hand-tied ribbon. It costs $50 to $500, ships free via USPS Ground Advantage, ships within 5 business days, and arrives in 7–12 business days. If you want more than one item, choose a Mystery Box.

What’s inside a Mystery Box?

Multiple gifts. Every Mystery Box contains multiple themed gifts, chosen for the size and theme you pick and hand-packed in one box. Smaller boxes hold a handful of gifts; bigger boxes hold a big haul of gifts. Contents are a surprise and change from order to order. Photos and theme descriptions illustrate a mood; they don’t guarantee specific products, brands, or exact item counts. Each box contains only new, genuine items purchased from authorized retailers. We don’t publish packing lists in advance; that’s the fun of it.

How do themes work?

You choose a theme from the list when you build your box, and we select items that fit that interest. Theme names describe a mood or hobby — they are not official brand shops, and Mystery Creations is not affiliated with or endorsed by any brand. Items are genuine, new products purchased from authorized retailers.

Is the price fixed? Is this a game of chance?

Every Mystery Box and every Mystery Gift is a fixed price for the size or price and theme you choose. What you pay is what you get: a curated themed gift. Applicable sales tax is added at checkout where it applies. There are no random prizes, odds, chances, rarity tiers, sweepstakes, or resale, trade-in, or buyback programs.

Can I choose specific items?

No — every Mystery Gift and Mystery Box is a surprise. You choose the price or box size and the theme, and we do the rest. If you have an allergy or restriction, write us on the contact form and we’ll do our best to accommodate.

What does the “who is this gift for” field do?

You can optionally describe the special person this is for. That description affects which mystery gift is picked. It is not a promise of a specific brand or item, and we can’t guarantee every request. The gift note is separate: it is a message for them and does not affect which gift is picked. If you leave either one blank, it stays blank.

Can I send a Mystery Box as a gift?

Yes. At checkout, enter the recipient’s U.S. shipping address so the Mystery Box goes to them. You can add an optional gift note (up to 100 characters) to tuck in the box, and optionally tell us a little about who it’s for so we can use that as a guide when we pack it.

Will I ever get the same item twice?

No. Ordering again? We track what we’ve sent you, so you’ll never get the same surprise item twice. We match on the email address you order with, so please use the same email each time.

Can I send it to a friend, and can it be gift wrapped?

Yes. Tick This is a gift — ship it to a friend in the order menu, add an optional note, and enter your friend’s U.S. address on the secure Stripe checkout step. You can also add optional gift wrap for $2.00. Gift orders ship with no price inside.

Can I include a gift note?

Yes. You can add an optional gift note (up to 100 characters). The gift note does not affect which mystery gift is picked — the description of the special person does. For a Mystery Box, we tuck the note in the box. For a Mystery Gift, we pass it to the retailer that ships the item and it is included when the retailer supports gift messages. If you leave the note blank, it stays blank.

How does shipping work?

Shipping is free on every order, within the United States only. Mystery Gifts (one single item) ship directly from a retailer to the recipient as a gift, with no price inside, and a gift usually arrives within 2–7 business days. Mystery Boxes (multiple gifts) are hand-packed at our shop in a quality gift box with a hand-tied ribbon and shipped via USPS Ground Advantage. Ships within 5 business days, arrives in 7–12 business days. Tracking is emailed to you when the box is on its way. Please enter a complete, accurate U.S. shipping address at checkout. Full details are in our shipping terms.

What is a Mystery Gift?

A Mystery Gift is one single surprise item, not a box, matched to the theme you pick, at $10, $15, $20, or $25. It ships directly from a retailer to the recipient as a gift, with no price inside, and gift wrap when available. Shipping is free, and it usually arrives within 2–7 business days. A Mystery Gift is a single item, not a Mystery Box. Want multiple gifts? Choose a Mystery Box from $50.

Can I change or cancel an order?

You may cancel for a full refund any time before your order ships. To cancel, email help@mymysterycreations.com from the address you ordered with and say cancel. Mystery Gifts are ordered from the retailer quickly, so write us right away. Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled, and all sales are final.

What’s your return and refund policy?

Because every Mystery Gift and Mystery Box is a surprise, there are no returns or exchanges. You may cancel for a full refund any time before your order ships. To cancel, email help@mymysterycreations.com from the address you ordered with and say cancel. Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled, and all sales are final. We’ll email tracking as soon as it ships. Refunds for cancelled orders go back to the original payment method. The full policy is in our refund terms.

What happens after my order ships?

Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled, and all sales are final. Because every Mystery Gift and Mystery Box is a surprise, there are no returns or exchanges. We’ll email tracking as soon as it ships.

How are payments handled?

Payments are processed securely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. You’ll pay the fixed price for the box or gift you chose, plus applicable sales tax at checkout where it applies, and shipping is always free. After payment, Stripe emails a receipt, and you’ll return to our thank-you page.

Are you affiliated with any brands?

No. Theme names describe interests only. Items are genuine, new products purchased from authorized retailers; Mystery Creations is not affiliated with or endorsed by any brand.

How do I get in touch?

Write us at help@mymysterycreations.com or use the contact form on this page. We also use that address for questions about an order, a return, or a review. We’re based in Laguna Vista, Texas.
