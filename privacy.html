Privacy policy

This policy describes how Mystery Creations, a business in the United States, handles personal information. Last updated November 14, 2026.

What we collect

When you place an order, Stripe collects the information needed to process payment and fulfill the box. We receive your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, a short note about who the box is for (if you add one), a gift note (if you add one), and the tier and theme you chose. We never see or store your card number. If you use the contact form, your mail app sends us the name, email, and message you enter. If you submit a review, we receive your first name, last initial, order email, theme, star rating, and review text. The order email is used only to confirm the review is tied to a real purchase and is not published. If we post the review, we publish your first name, last initial, theme, box, rating, review text, and the date. If you join the launch list, we receive your email address and, if you choose one, a theme you are interested in. If you write to us about a partnership or promotion, we receive the email and message you send to marketing@mymysterycreations.com. Your box size and theme in progress may also be saved in your browser’s local storage so the page can remember them. This site does not require a customer account.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe’s use of your information is governed by Stripe’s privacy policy. We do not see or store your full card number.

How we use information

  • To fulfill and ship your box, and to provide customer support.
  • To send order, receipt, and UPS tracking emails.
  • To answer questions you send us.
  • To verify review submissions against real orders and, if approved, to publish them.
  • To email launch and product updates if you join the launch list.
  • To discuss promotional partnerships if you write to our marketing address.
  • To keep records required for taxes and accounting.

We use this information only to fulfill, ship, and support your order, to send the updates you asked for, and as required by law. Launch-list emails are used only for launch and product updates. You can unsubscribe or ask us to delete your launch-list email any time by writing to help@mymysterycreations.com.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with Stripe (to process payment) and UPS (to ship your box), or when the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not include a third-party analytics script. If you arrive from a campaign link, the site may temporarily remember the source of your visit in your browser’s session storage so we can understand which posts led you here. That information is cleared when you close the tab and is not sent to a third-party analytics service unless this policy is updated to say so. If analytics or marketing cookies are added later, this policy will be updated and, where required, a consent notice will be shown.

Your choices

You can clear this site’s saved box selection by clearing local storage for the site in your browser. To unsubscribe from the launch list, or to ask about, correct, or delete information we hold — including a launch-list email — write to help@mymysterycreations.com.

Children

This store is intended for customers 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Privacy questions: help@mymysterycreations.com.
