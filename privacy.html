Privacy policy

Placeholder policy for Mystery Creations. This will be completed before paid orders are accepted. Last updated November 12, 2026.

What we collect

When you browse this static site, we do not run a store account system. Your box size and theme selection may be saved in your browser’s local storage so the page can remember it. If you use the contact form, your mail app sends us the name, email, and message you enter. When Stripe Checkout is connected, Stripe will collect your name, email, payment details, and shipping address in order to process the order. We do not see or store your full card number.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. Stripe’s use of your information is governed by Stripe’s privacy policy. We receive the information needed to fulfill your order (typically name, email, shipping address, and what you bought).

How we use information

  • To fulfill and ship mystery boxes, including sharing the shipping address with our packing team and carriers such as UPS.
  • To send order and tracking emails.
  • To answer questions you send us.
  • To keep basic records required for taxes and accounting.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with services that help us run the store (for example Stripe, email, and shipping carriers) or when the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

This version of the site does not include a third-party analytics script. If analytics or marketing cookies are added later, this policy will be updated and, where required, a consent notice will be shown.

Your choices

You can clear this site’s saved box selection by clearing local storage for the site in your browser. To ask about, correct, or delete information we hold after an order, email hello@mysterycreations.example.
