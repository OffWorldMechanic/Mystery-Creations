The store

Mystery Creations sells two kinds of themed surprises at fixed prices. Mystery Gifts ($5, $10, $15, $20, or $25) are one single surprise item (not a box) matched to the theme you choose, shipped directly from a retailer to the recipient as a gift, with no price inside. Mystery Boxes ($50 to $500) contain multiple themed gifts that we hand-pack at our shop in a quality gift box with a hand-tied ribbon and ship to the U.S. address you provide at checkout.
